How it works

📷 Image / camera→YuNet face detector→Align eyes + tight crop→DDAMFN (ONNX)→7 emotions → sentiment

Both networks run in the browser with ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly). The emotion model is DDAMFN: a MixedFeatureNet backbone pretrained on MS-Celeb-1M faces with a dual-direction attention head, 4.19 M parameters. The default generalist was fine-tuned on RAF-DB + FER2013 together. Its explanations use occlusion sensitivity: hide part of the face and measure how much the prediction drops.

Results (test sets, evaluated once)

ModelRAF-DBFER2013CK+ (unseen)
Specialist (RAF-DB only)91.07%55.29%77.02%
Generalist (this page's default)90.81%69.00%78.96%
4-model ensemble (offline)91.98%71.72%77.56%

RAF-DB: 3,068 real-world test faces. FER2013: 7,178 grayscale 48×48 faces (human agreement ≈ 65%). CK+ was never used in training.

Highlights

  • Near state of the art on RAF-DB with ~10× fewer parameters than POSTER++ (92.2%).
  • Multi-dataset training lifted FER2013 from 55% to 69% and also improved unseen CK+.
  • Robustness tested against blur, low light, noise, compression and occlusion. A mask hurts more than sunglasses.
  • Calibrated confidence scores (temperature scaling, ECE 3.8% → 1.4%).
  • Deployment: ONNX export, 1.9× faster than PyTorch on CPU with identical predictions.

Limitations & responsible use

Facial expression is not the same as felt emotion. Fear and Disgust are the weakest classes, and accuracy drops with heavy occlusion, blur or extreme head poses. This is a research demo and must not be used to judge, hire or surveil people.
